Delete your account

Rabbi Gips Podcasts · Birkas Shiur

This page is for the app Rabbi Gips Podcasts (also called Birkas Shiur) on Google Play and the App Store, published by Rav Gips Shiur – שיעורי מורינו הרב י. צ. גיפס שליט"א. Signing in is optional; if you made an account, you can delete it and its data at any time.

Option 1 – in the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap the menu ☰ (top left).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2 – without the app

Email us from the email address you signed up with, and write “Delete my account”. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Email birkasshiur@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address and password
  • Your account ID and notification token
  • Favorites and listening history saved to your account

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. App usage statistics are anonymous (not linked to your account) and are deleted automatically by Google Analytics after 14 months. Shiurim you downloaded and settings stay on your phone until you delete the app.
