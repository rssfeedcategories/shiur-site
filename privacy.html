Privacy Policy

Birkas Shiur app (Rabbi Gips – Birkas Avrohom) · Last updated October 2026

What the app does

The app plays and downloads Torah shiurim and shows their source pages. You can use all of it without an account.

Information kept on your phone

Your listening history, where you stopped, favorites, downloads and settings are stored only on your phone.

Signing in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from that service. We use them only to back up your history, positions, favorites and downloads list so they can be restored on a new phone. This is stored with Google Firebase.

Reminders and notifications

Daily reminders are set on your phone and do not send any information to us. If you allow notifications, a device token is used only to deliver announcements about new shiurim.

Content

Audio and source pages are loaded from several websites. These see standard technical information such as your IP address when the app loads files from them.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track your location or contacts.

Deleting your information

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. If you signed in and want your backup deleted, email us and we'll remove it.
