Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Everything works without signing in.
  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? – we'll email you a 6-digit code.
  • Delete your account: Menu → Delete account (when signed in), or email us.
  • Listen offline: tap the download button on any shiur.
  • View the Gemara / sefer page: open a shiur and tap View PDF.
